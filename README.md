**Figure 3** - Big mandate, per capita challenge: Per capita marine conservation commitment, calculated as the total area of established marine conservation areas relative to national population size. Conservation area coverage was obtained from Protected Planet, population estimates from the R package rnaturalearth, and exclusive economic zone (EEZ) areas from the Marine Regions database. The analysis was restricted to countries with populations exceeding one million to reduce the influence of small island states on per capita comparisons. The top 25 countries by this metric are shown.
